## Termux

The terminal renderer and touch interaction were designed with reference to the open-source Termux terminal implementation, especially its wcwidth-based glyph sizing, transcript scrolling, and xterm SGR mouse-wheel behavior.

Source: https://github.com/termux/termux-app
License: GPL-3.0

No Termux source files or binaries are bundled in this project.
